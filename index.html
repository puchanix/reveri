The evidence

Studied for decades. Now validated at digital scale.

In December 2025, an analysis of 282,893 Reveri stress sessions across 84,395 members was published in npj Digital Medicine. Here is what it found — including the parts that complicate the story.

−1.5

Average drop in self-rated stress within a single session, on a ten-point scale — consistent across members' first ten sessions.

0.75

Effect size (Cohen's d) for that within-session reduction. In behavioural research, that is a large effect.

Interactive

Sessions where members answered questions produced significantly greater relief than ones they only listened to. It isn't a stylistic preference — it's the part that does the work.

npj Digital Medicine · 2025

Effects of app-delivered self-hypnosis on stress management

The study above. Tran, Saperia, Neri, Jo, Kim, Zewde, Nouriani, Kinderman, Jagielo, Faerman, Maldonado & Spiegel.

Read the paper →

The Lancet · 2000

Adjunctive non-pharmacological analgesia for invasive medical procedures

A randomised trial in which self-hypnosis reduced pain and drug use during invasive procedures. Lang, Benotsch, Fick, Lutgendorf, Berbaum, Berbaum, Logan & Spiegel.

Read the trial →

What it doesn't show. The 2025 analysis is observational — it looked at what happened in real sessions, without a control group, so it can't prove the sessions caused the change on their own. It also found that relief was consistent from one session to the next: the tenth session works like the first. That's reliability, not a promise that the effect grows by itself. We'd rather tell you that than let you find it.

Straight answers

What Reveri is, and what it isn't.

It isn't Dr. Spiegel live

Every session is guided by an AI built on Dr. Spiegel's method and speaking in his voice. He designed it, trained it and reviews how it works — but he isn't on the line, and he doesn't hear your sessions. What "remembers you" is the software, kept private to your account.

It works alongside your care, not instead of it

Reveri is a wellness product. It is not a substitute for medical care, and nothing here is a reason to change or stop anything your doctor has prescribed.

It doesn't work identically for everyone

How people respond to hypnosis genuinely varies. That's why your first session begins by finding out how you respond — and why every session after is tuned to it.

It isn't crisis support

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line. Reveri is not built for that moment, and we would rather say so plainly.
