The part people remember

Everyone has a place they go.

Partway into a session, David asks you to imagine yourself floating — somewhere safe, somewhere yours. Almost nobody picks the same place twice.

Members have described more than 7,500 of them. A lake in Michigan. The back of a boat. A hammock at a grandmother's house. Nearly two in three involve water, and no two are written the same way.

It's the moment members bring up unprompted more than any other — and the clearest sign that the session is theirs rather than something played at them.

The evidence

Studied for decades. Now validated at digital scale.

In December 2025, an analysis of 282,893 Reveri stress sessions across 84,395 members was published in npj Digital Medicine. Here is what it found, including the parts that complicate the story.

−1.5

Average drop in self-rated stress within a single session, on a ten-point scale — consistent across members' first ten sessions.

0.75

Effect size (Cohen's d) for that within-session reduction. In behavioural research, that is a large effect.

Interactive

Sessions where members answered questions produced significantly greater relief than ones they only listened to.

npj Digital Medicine · 2025

Effects of app-delivered self-hypnosis on stress management

The study above. Tran, Saperia, Neri, Jo, Kim, Zewde, Nouriani, Kinderman, Jagielo, Faerman, Maldonado & Spiegel.

Read the paper →
The Lancet · 2000

Adjunctive non-pharmacological analgesia for invasive medical procedures: a randomised trial

A randomised trial in which self-hypnosis reduced pain and drug use during invasive procedures. Lang, Benotsch, Fick, Lutgendorf, Berbaum, Berbaum, Logan & Spiegel.

Read the trial →

What it doesn't show. The 2025 analysis is observational — it looked at what happened in real sessions, without a control group, so it can't prove the sessions caused the change on their own. It also found that relief was consistent session to session rather than compounding over time. We'd rather tell you that than let you find it. What Reveri is, and what it isn't.

It works alongside your care, not instead of it

Reveri is a wellness product. It is not a substitute for medical care, and nothing here is a reason to change or stop anything your doctor has prescribed.

It doesn't work identically for everyone

Hypnotic responsiveness genuinely varies, and our own published analysis found it predicts how much relief people get. The free test tells you where you're likely to land before you pay.

It isn't crisis support

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line. Reveri is not built for that moment, and we would rather say so plainly.
