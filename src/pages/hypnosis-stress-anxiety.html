If this is you

When stress won't let you think straight.

Stress isn't a discipline problem, and it isn't solved by trying harder. It lives in the mind, and it shows up in the body: tight shoulders, shallow breath, the night you can't sleep, the pain that gets worse. In a state of focused attention you can separate the problem from the physical alarm — and once they're apart, both get smaller.

How Dr. Spiegel works with it

One practice. His method, applied to your situation.

Reveri isn't a library of recordings or a separate program for each condition. It's Dr. Spiegel's method, delivered by an AI he trained, that asks what's going on and chooses what to do about it — and remembers, so the next session continues rather than starts over.

1

He finds out how you respond

A short test — a few minutes — that tells him how your mind takes suggestion and how to work with you.

2

He separates the problem from the alarm

Dr. Spiegel's signature approach: you picture what is worrying you on a screen, at a distance, while your body floats — so the thought and the physical stress are no longer welded together. From there he works on the specific thing: a week that won't let go, a conversation you're dreading, the loop at bedtime.

3

He tells you what shifted, and what's next

You rate your stress before and after. Then he tells you what he wants to try next time and why. If your stress is costing you sleep, or amplifying pain, he says so and works on that too.

Questions

Straight answers about hypnosis and stress & anxiety.

Can hypnosis help with anxiety?

Yes. It is one of the best-supported non-drug approaches, and Reveri's own peer-reviewed study of 282,893 sessions found a large, consistent within-session reduction in stress.

Is this the same as meditation?

No. Meditation trains you to observe. Hypnosis is a state of focused attention in which you actively change how you relate to something. Many members do both; they are different tools.

What about panic or a crisis?

Reveri is not crisis support. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.

How fast does it work?

Most of the change in the study arrived within the first five minutes of a session. You will know by the end of your first one.
