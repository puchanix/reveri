Questions

Straight answers about hypnosis and sleep.

Can I hypnotize myself to sleep?

That is essentially what a sleep session is: a guided way into the state your body needs to let sleep happen. Dr. Spiegel's AI does the guiding; you do the drifting.

Does it help with insomnia?

Hypnosis has improved sleep outcomes in the majority of studies that measured it, and it addresses the anxiety-about-sleep that makes insomnia stick. It is not a treatment for a medical sleep disorder; talk to your doctor about those.

What if I wake up at 3 a.m.?

There is a session for exactly that: getting back to sleep. Most members who use it fall back asleep within minutes.

Is it safe to use every night?

Yes. There is nothing to build a tolerance to and no side effects. Many members use it nightly.
