Research & safety

The science is settled. Hypnosis works.

It is one of the most studied mind-body interventions in medicine, with mechanisms visible on brain imaging and results in controlled trials for pain, stress and sleep. Dr. David Spiegel has spent fifty years at Stanford producing much of that evidence. This page is what it shows — and what it doesn't.

What 84,395 members showed.

An analysis of 282,893 stress sessions, published in December 2025 by Tran, Saperia, Neri, Jo, Kim, Zewde, Nouriani, Kinderman, Jagielo, Faerman, Maldonado and Spiegel.

−1.5

Average drop in self-rated stress within a single session, on a ten-point scale — consistent across members' first ten sessions.

0.75

Effect size (Cohen's d) for that within-session reduction. In behavioural research, that is a large effect.

Interactive

Sessions in which members answered questions produced significantly greater relief than listening alone. The interaction is what does the work.

Read the paper →

What it doesn't show. The study is observational: it measured what happened in real sessions, without a control group, so it can't prove the sessions caused the change on their own. It also found that relief was consistent from one session to the next rather than growing — the tenth session works like the first. That is reliability, not a promise that the effect compounds by itself. We would rather tell you that than let you find it.

By topic

What the evidence says, subject by subject.

Short summaries written by Dr. Spiegel's research team, each with references.

Safety

Done responsibly, hypnosis is safer than many medications taken for the same concerns. The evidence of risk is scant and mostly anecdotal.

The brain during hypnosis

Less monitoring of the senses, less mind-wandering, and reasoning that adapts to the suggestion — visible on imaging.

Hypnotizability

A stable trait, as consistent as IQ. Most people score as hypnotizable, and even those who score low benefit.

Hypnosis vs. placebo

Both involve expectancy; they are not the same thing. Hypnosis involves learning to alter perception and physiology deliberately.

Genetics

Several genes are linked to hypnotizability, and hypnosis sessions activate genetic programs tied to immune response and reduced inflammation.

Pain

One of the best drug-free treatments for pain; reduces sensation and the distress that comes with it.

Stress & anxiety

Reduces symptoms, potentially more effectively than other common treatments, and normalises stress hormones.

Sleep

Improves sleep quality, reduces insomnia-related distress and increases deep sleep. Safer than sleep medication.

Smoking

Likely more effective than common drugs and counselling for cessation, and enhances them when combined.
