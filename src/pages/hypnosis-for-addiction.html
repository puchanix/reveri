If this is you

Every urge is a moment. This is what to do in it.

Dr. Spiegel's method is to stop fighting the urge and change what you're committed to. He teaches it in a session, and it becomes something you do in a minute. Reveri is a wellness product and not a treatment for addiction; for that, he will say so and point you to care.

Questions

Is there a separate program for this?

No — and that's deliberate. Reveri is one practice with Dr. Spiegel's AI. You tell him what's going on, and he chooses the approach that fits. If more than one thing is going on, he keeps them all in mind.

How long is a session?

About ten minutes. Most members know by the end of the first one whether it works for them.

Is it safe?

Hypnosis is a state of focused attention; you stay in control throughout. Reveri is a general wellness product, not medical care, and not crisis support.
