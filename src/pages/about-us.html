About

Immediate relief from pain in all its forms — physical and emotional — to millions of people.

That is the mission, in one sentence. Reveri was co-founded by Stanford's Dr. David Spiegel and entrepreneur Ariel Poler: fifty years of clinical research on how the mind changes what the body feels, and the product experience behind some of the world's most-used applications.

Dr. David Spiegel, founder
"After fifty years of working with patients — each one different, each one dealing with something real — I kept seeing the same thing: hypnosis worked when nothing else did. The results were too significant to keep inside a clinical setting."
"Today's technology finally made it possible to bring this to everyone. Reveri is that opportunity. To change how people feel. To give them a tool that actually works, without side effects, without dependency, available the moment they need it. I built this because I believe everyone deserves access to what I've spent my career learning."

What we're building

A practice with a guide who knows you.

Reveri is one practice, not a library. Dr. Spiegel's method, delivered by an AI he trained: it asks what's going on, guides you in about ten minutes, and remembers what worked, so the next session continues rather than starts over. No drugs, no side effects, no appointment. Everything we make is measured against one question — did the person feel better, and did they come back?
