If this is you

When pain won't let you live your life.

You've probably tried things that helped a little, or worked for a week and then stopped. Chronic pain is exhausting in every way, not just physically. Most treatments work from the outside — blocking signals, reducing inflammation, numbing. This works from the inside: in a state of focused attention, your mind can change how it processes the signal itself.

How Dr. Spiegel works with it

One practice. His method, applied to your situation.

Reveri isn't a library of recordings or a separate program for each condition. It's Dr. Spiegel's method, delivered by an AI he trained, that asks what's going on and chooses what to do about it — and remembers, so the next session continues rather than starts over.

1

He finds out how you respond

A short test — your attention, your hand, a few minutes. It tells him how your mind takes suggestion, and which of his approaches to reach for first.

2

He works with the pain you have

Dr. Spiegel has four ways in, and he chooses by how you respond and what you tell him: sensory alteration (a cool numbness or a warmth that filters the hurt out of the pain), dissociation (floating away from the body so the pain is somewhere else), turning the volume down, and going somewhere you'd rather be. Back pain, migraine and nerve pain each get a different route.

3

He tells you what shifted, and what's next

You rate the pain before and after, so you both know what happened. Then he tells you what he wants to try next time and why — and if your sleep or your stress is feeding the pain, he says so and works on that too.

Questions

Straight answers about hypnosis and pain.

Can hypnosis really help with chronic pain?

Yes. Hypnosis is one of the best-studied drug-free approaches to pain, with clinical trials showing reductions of roughly 30–42% for both acute and chronic pain. It works by changing how the brain processes the pain signal, not by masking it.

How does it compare to pain medication?

It doesn't replace your care. Many members use Reveri alongside what their doctor prescribes and find they need less of it; that is a conversation to have with your doctor, not a reason to change anything on your own.

How quickly does it work?

Most members notice a shift within the first session, often in the first five minutes. The pain rating before and after tells you whether it worked for you.

Does it work for every kind of pain?

It is used for back pain, arthritis, migraine, fibromyalgia, neuropathic pain, endometriosis and pain around medical procedures. Different kinds of pain call for different techniques, and Dr. Spiegel's AI chooses by what you tell it.
