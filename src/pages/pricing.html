Why is the first session free?

Because most people know after one session whether this works for them, and we would rather you found out before paying. There is no card required to start on the web.

Do prices vary by country?

Yes. App store and web prices are set per region. What you see here is the US price in dollars; the exact price in your currency appears at checkout and in the app store before you confirm.

How do I cancel?

The same way you started: through the App Store, Google Play, or your account page if you subscribed online. In the app, Manage your Membership is in the More menu. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for.

Is there a refund?

Refunds are handled by the platform you paid through — Apple, Google or Stripe. Write to support@reveri.com if you run into trouble.
