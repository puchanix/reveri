How a session works

Ten quiet minutes. He does the rest.

Sessions are a conversation, not a recording. David asks, you answer, and where it goes next depends on what you said.

1

He asks what's going on

Where it hurts, how bad it is right now, what you've already tried today. A number from one to ten is enough to start.

2

He guides you down

Eyes closed, somewhere you won't be interrupted. He talks you into a state of highly focused concentration and puts it to work on the thing you named.

3

You say what changed

One tap at the end. That answer is what he opens with next time — which is how the sessions stop being separate and start compounding.

Straight answers

What Reveri is, and what it isn't.

It works alongside your care, not instead of it

Reveri is a wellness product. It is not a substitute for medical care, and nothing here is a reason to change or stop anything your doctor has prescribed. Keep your doctor in the loop.

It doesn't work identically for everyone

Hypnotic responsiveness genuinely varies. Most people feel something meaningful in the first session; some need a few. The free test tells you where you're likely to land before you pay.

If a session meets real distress, it stops and helps

Sessions are built to recognise when someone is in crisis and to respond with real resources rather than carrying on. We explain exactly how on the Science & Safety page.
